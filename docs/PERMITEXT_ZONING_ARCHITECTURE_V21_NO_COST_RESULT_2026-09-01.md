## Stronger deterministic enforcement

V2 accepted obligation text anywhere in the answer. V2.1 additionally supports:

- grouped requirements, so every independent part of a boundary must appear;
- uncertainty-scoped requirements, so an unresolved fact must be retained as uncertainty rather than merely mentioned as a rule;
- source-bound supported-point requirements;
- rejection when a proposition is attached to the wrong source even if the correct citation appears elsewhere; and
- contradiction patterns for narrow, observed semantic reversals such as treating an allocation exception as an increase to total floor area.

FAR limits and commercial-to-residential mappings are now read only from validated structured table rows. Missing or malformed controlling rows, missing governing lot-coverage percentages, and missing controlling source identifiers stop at the evidence-readiness boundary. Incidental district names or decimal values in prose cannot manufacture a legal numeric limit. Project facts marked unknown do not suppress unresolved-fact obligations.

These controls run on the first generated answer before delivery and again after the one permitted bounded patch. They do not create a second repair or permit a full-answer rewrite.

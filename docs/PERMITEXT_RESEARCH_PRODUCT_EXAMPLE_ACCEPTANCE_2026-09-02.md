## Purpose

This benchmark converts the owner's real Permitext and ChatGPT screenshots into permanent product requirements. The screenshots are examples of useful answer organization, not legal authority. Every legal claim must still come from the exact enacted Permitext corpus and edition identified for the question.

The machine-readable cases are in `permitext-sync-server/evals/research-product-example-cases.json`. The permanent contract is `permitext-sync-server/tests/research-product-example-acceptance-contract.mjs`.

## Required answer behavior

- Lead with the usable answer, not retrieval-process narration.
- Put a compact human-readable citation next to each material rule or value.
- Use an Item / Requirement / Authority table when an open-ended design question has several supported parallel requirements.
- Use a comparison table only for genuinely shared features supported by the assembled evidence.
- Give the number first for a numeric question, followed by its scope and material exceptions.
- Give a one-paragraph answer when the user asks for a short explanation.
- Confirm or correct the edition directly on an edition follow-up; never mix similarly numbered 2014 and 2022 provisions.
- Separate enacted code, referenced standards, agency requirements, and noncontrolling guidance. Never fill an unsupplied authority from memory.

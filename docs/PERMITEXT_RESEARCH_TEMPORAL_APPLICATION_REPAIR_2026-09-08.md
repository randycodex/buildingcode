The [previous live review](PERMITEXT_RESEARCH_PROMPT_COMPACTION_AND_EXPANSION_2026-09-08.md) found that ZR-18 quoted the governing date condition correctly but then applied Section 77-02 conclusively from a lot's 2026 assembly year. This repair separates the rule from the facts needed to apply it. Project-workflow testing remains deferred until the owner explicitly asks.

## What changed

The Zoning compiler now includes a source-derived application obligation when the selected governing text supplies Section 77-02's existence-date condition. It records whether supplied facts establish that the lot existed, did not exist, or remain unresolved on the applicable date. A formation year by itself cannot resolve an unidentified applicable amendment date. The existing draft and repair prompts receive that obligation through the deterministic context; the compiler checks material application claims in the main answer and individual supporting points.

The guard addresses both an unsupported portion-by-portion application and a temporal disqualification from Section 77-11 inferred from formation year alone. The relevant rule remains in official [77-02](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-7/77-02) and [77-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-7/77-11). The main answer can still definitively reject the proposition that majority area alone automatically determines the whole lot's use rules. The separate FAR mechanism remains separate.

Recognized declarations can establish the date condition, including explicit relative timing and formation/amendment dates. Years are intervals: two dates supplied only as the same year do not establish order. Conflicting dates, invalid calendar dates, unresolved facts, and quoted rules do not become established premises. The check is deliberately bounded to recognized source conditions, declarations and application wording; it is not a complete semantic verifier for arbitrary legal prose.

The compiler version is `20260908-temporal-application-v23`. Source evidence, model routing, reasoning settings, output limits and spending guards remain intact. No expected answer is supplied to the provider. No automatic replacement of a generated conclusion was added.

## Purpose and boundary

The retained 30-case diagnostic exposed six rubrics whose required concepts should be checked against the actual question. This audit distinguishes material answer requirements from collateral detail, duplicate arithmetic, and evidence prescriptions that are narrower than the legal issue requires.

This document does not change the frozen cohort, retroactively rescore the retained result, authorize a paid run, enable public Zoning Research, or approve a project conclusion. Any accepted change must be made in a separately versioned successor after owner disposition.

## Proposed scope dispositions

### `zr-rules-of-construction`

Question asked: how to resolve a conflict between enacted text and an illustration or summary table.

Disposition: **trim one collateral concept**.

- Retain that enacted text controls over a conflicting caption, illustration, summary table, or illustrative table.
- Retain identification of ZR 12-01.
- Remove the separate requirement that the particular controls the general. That is enacted text in the same section, but it does not answer the conflict described in the question.

The retained answer directly answered the question and cited the controlling provision. It should not fail for omitting a different construction rule.

### `zr-candidate-b1-nonconforming-warehouse-enlargement`

Question asked: whether a stated 5,000-square-foot addition to an existing 20,000-square-foot warehouse receives an automatic 25 percent enlargement.

Disposition: **retain the central percentage; remove one duplicate margin**.

- Retain `5,000 / 20,000 = 25%` because it verifies that the proposal matches the percentage invoked by the question.
- Retain the limited BSA-path calculation only as a conditional numerical screen, never as eligibility or approval.
- Remove the separate requirement to state `9,000 - 5,000 = 4,000`. Stating that the proposal is below the conditional 9,000-square-foot ceiling is sufficient; the unused margin does not change the legal answer.

### `zr-candidate-b1-r7a-r8a-weighted-far`

Question asked: whether the higher R8A FAR can be used for the whole split-district lot and whether the 110,000-square-foot proposal fits.

Disposition: **retain the proposal comparison; remove duplicate arithmetic**.

- Retain the weighted-FAR method, adjusted 5.212 FAR, 104,240-square-foot maximum, proposed 5.50 FAR, and 5,760-square-foot excess.
- Remove the separate alternative direct-area check `(8,000 x 4.00) + (12,000 x 6.02)`. It proves the same maximum already established by the required weighted calculation.

### `zr-candidate-b1-c6-2-office-residential-conversion`

Question asked: whether 100 converted apartments fit the preliminary density limit and how much recreation space is required.

Disposition: **retain the existing-FAR comparison; no scope trim proposed**.

- Retain `90,000 / 15,000 = 6.00 FAR` because the no-enlargement fact and narrow-street 6.02 ceiling make the existing building's FAR material to the conversion comparison.
- Retain the preliminary unit calculation and the 3 percent/conditional 2 percent recreation calculations.
- Continue to distinguish a preliminary numerical result from legal conversion approval.

The retained answer was substantively useful but omitted the concise 6.00 FAR calculation. This is a real answer omission, not rubric overreach.

### `zr-candidate-b1-city-of-yes-transition`

Question asked: whether the stated filing, permit, and foundation facts allow continuation under pre-City-of-Yes zoning.

Disposition: **merge overlapping historical-text requirements and remove duplicate percentage arithmetic**.

- Retain the distinction between the general ZR 11-331 completed-foundation route and the separate ZR 11-333(9) transition route.
- Retain the need to verify DOB's qualifying approval and complete zoning analysis.
- Replace the overlapping historical-text concepts with one material requirement: distinguish the current transition provision from the verified pre-December 5, 2024 substantive zoning provisions needed to determine what rights the transition preserves.
- Do not separately require an amendment adoption/effective-date recital unless the selected evidence establishes that fact and the distinction changes the answer.
- Remove separate requirements to calculate `100% - 60% = 40%` and a `40-percentage-point` shortfall. Saying that 60 percent does not satisfy an all-foundations-complete condition is sufficient.

The retained answer's failure to require verified historical substantive text remains a real omission. The two 40 percent calculations and a free-standing amendment-date recital are collateral.

### `zr-candidate-b1-mih-historical-zoning-lot`

Question asked: whether the numerical small-development thresholds establish an MIH exception when the current tax lots were combined after the reported MIH establishment date.

Disposition: **retain the historical-lot inquiry; remove unnecessary margins and over-specific record language**.

- Retain that every exception element must be established, including the zoning lot's existence when the applicable MIH area was established.
- Retain the distinction between tax lots and a zoning lot, and between a current Appendix F map and the official historical establishment record.
- Require official historical zoning-lot evidence, including any relevant recorded declaration or legal description, without making one particular metes-and-bounds record formulation the only acceptable proof.
- Retain `8 <= 10` and `10,000 <= 12,500`; remove separate requirements to state the unused margins of two units and 2,500 square feet.

The retained answer correctly withheld the exception conclusion. It should remain responsible for the historical map/lot distinction, but not for redundant threshold margins or an unnecessarily exclusive records formulation.

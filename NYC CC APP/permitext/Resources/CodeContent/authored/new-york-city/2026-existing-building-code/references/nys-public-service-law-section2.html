2. Definitions.

UTILITY COMPANY OR PUBLIC UTILITY COMPANY. The term "utility company" or "public utility company" is used to avoid repetitions in a provision applying to one or more persons or corporations operating an agency or agencies for public service, and who or which is or are subject to the jurisdiction, supervision and regulations prescribed by or pursuant to provisions of this chapter other than article 11; such term being so used only as a general term descriptive of such a person or corporation.

UTILITY CORPORATION OR PUBLIC UTILITY CORPORATION. The term "utility corporation" or "public utility corporation" is an incorporated utility company.

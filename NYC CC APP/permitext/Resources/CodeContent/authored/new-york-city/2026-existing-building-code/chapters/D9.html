APPENDIX D

LODGING HOUSES

Enacted, not yet effective. Effective July 17, 2027.

EBC D901 Alterations to and maintenance of lodging houses. Alterations to multiple dwellings

classified as LHs shall comply with the provisions of the New York City Existing Building Code,
the provisions of Section 66 of the MDL, and the rules of the department of housing preservation
and development in relation to the conduct and maintenance of lodging houses.

Official enacted source: Local Law 33 of 2026

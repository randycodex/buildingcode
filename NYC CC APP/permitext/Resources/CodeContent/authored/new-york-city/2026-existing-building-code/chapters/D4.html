APPENDIX D

PRE-1968 HEREAFTER ERECTED FIREPROOF MULTIPLE DWELLINGS

Enacted, not yet effective. Effective July 17, 2027.

EBC D401 Applicability. Alterations, additions, repairs and changes of occupancy of fireproof multiple

dwellings erected prior to December 6, 1968, and classified as HAEA (1929-1968) and HAEB
(1929-1968) shall comply with the provisions of this code and the additional provisions of Chapter
D3.

EBC D402 Rooms in basements and cellars

D402.1 New rooms in basements or cellars (MDL 34(6)). Rooms created on or after the
effective date of the local law that added this section in basements and cellars within dwelling
units in fireproof multiple dwellings classified as HAEA (1929-1968) or HAEB (1929-1968)
shall comply with the requirements of Section D305.4.1.
D402.2. Existing rooms in basements and cellars (MDL 34(5), 34(6)). For the purposes of
establishing the lawful conditions of existing rooms in basements or cellars within dwelling
units in fireproof multiple dwellings classified as HAEA (1929-1968) or HAEB (1929-1968),
refer to Section D305.4.1 or Sections 34(1) to 34(4) of the MDL.

Official enacted source: Local Law 33 of 2026

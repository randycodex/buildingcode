APPENDIX D

CONVERTED DWELLINGS

Enacted, not yet effective. Effective July 17, 2027.

EBC D601 Applicability. Alterations, additions, repairs, and changes of occupancy of a multiple

dwelling classified as HCA, HACA, HCB, and HACB, and conversion of a building originally
erected before December 6, 1968, as a one- or two-family dwelling to a EBC-CDA, shall comply
with the provisions of this code and the additional provisions in this Chapter.
D601.1 Dwellings erected after December 6, 1968. This Chapter shall not apply to
conversions of buildings originally erected on or after December 6, 1968, as one- or two-family
dwellings. Such buildings shall be classified as HAEA (1968-2008) or HAEA (2008+), as
applicable.
D601.2 Conversion to HAEA (MDL 9(3)). Where multiple dwellings classified as HCA,
HACA, HCB, and HACB are converted to HAEA (2008+), they shall comply with the
provisions of Chapter D3 pursuant to Section D303, and the provisions of this Chapter shall
not apply.

EBC D602 Alterations to existing converted dwellings. Alterations to existing multiple dwellings

classified as HCA, HACA, HCB, and HACB shall comply with this code and Sections D602.1
through D602.4.
D602.1 Increase in units/additions. Such a dwelling shall not be increased: (i) in number of
units, (ii) in height, (iii) by rooftop addition (other than a stair or elevator bulkhead), (iv) in
stories, or (v) by horizontal addition so as to exceed by 25 percent or more the area on any
story which such building occupied at the time of its conversions; unless all of the following
are met:
1. A second means of egress shall be provided that is directly accessible from within every
dwelling unit in the building including those outside the work area, which shall be either a
system of outside fire-escapes, constructed, and arranged as provided in Section D306.3, a
fire-stair, or a fire-tower. Where such second means of egress discharges into a rear yard
of less than 30 feet (9144 mm), compliance with Section D303.6.12 shall be required;
2. Any existing first means of egress that extends to the roof shall be maintained, provided
where the number of stories is increased, the stairs shall be a minimum of 36 inches (914
mm). A bulkhead shall be provided in lieu of an existing scuttle when the number of stories
increases to any number exceeding 3 stories above a basement;
3. Sprinklers shall be provided throughout the building in accordance with Section 903 of
the New York City Building Code;
4. The building shall not become a high-rise building, and shall comply with the height and
area limitations of Chapter 5 of the New York City Building Code not to exceed 6 stories in
height above grade plane, as such term height is defined in the New York City Building
Code;
5. The building shall have a fire alarm system in accordance with Section 907 of the New
York City Building Code;
6. Carbon monoxide and smoke alarms shall be provided throughout the building in
accordance with Chapter 9 of the New York City Building Code;
7. The building is not a frame building;
8. No portion of the building constituting an addition shall contain Class B dwelling units;
9. The public hall and stair partitions shall be constructed as 1-hour fire barriers and the
soffit of the stairs, if combustible, fire retarded in accordance with Section E402 of
Appendix E;
10. Doors opening to the public hall and stair shall be in compliance with Chapter 7 of the
New York City Building Code for opening protectives; and
11. Notwithstanding any contrary requirements of the New York City Existing Building
Code, the stairs need to be separated from the public halls only at the enlarged portions of
the building.
Exceptions. The following exceptions may not be utilized as part of the same alteration
project:
1. Penthouse additions. Only the following shall be required where a building is being
vertically enlarged with a penthouse in connection with a Class A dwelling unit below,
and such penthouse is not considered a story in accordance with Section 504.3 of the
New York City Building Code:
1.1. Any existing first means of egress that extends to the roof shall be maintained
and extended to the roof, and shall be permitted of the same width as the existing
stairs. A bulkhead shall be provided in lieu of an existing scuttle.
1.2. Sprinklers shall be provided in accordance with Section 903 of the New York
City Building Code throughout the dwelling unit, including the penthouse addition,
and shall extend to protect the public hall and stair at all levels of such dwelling
unit.
1.3. Carbon monoxide and smoke alarms shall be provided throughout the
dwelling unit, including the penthouse, in accordance with Chapter 9 of the New
York City Building Code.
1.4. The building is not a frame building.
2. One-story horizontal additions. Only the following shall be required where not
more than 1 story is being horizontally enlarged by 50 percent or less of the area on
such story:
2.1. The addition shall be provided with sprinklers in accordance with Section 903
of the New York City Building Code, including any dwelling units in connection
therewith.
2.2. The addition shall be provided with carbon monoxide and smoke alarms in
accordance with Chapter 9 of the New York City Building Code, including any
dwelling units in connection therewith.
2.3. The building is not a frame building.
3. Increase in number of dwelling units by 50 percent or less. Only the following
shall be required where the number of dwelling units is increased by 50 percent or less
by subdivision of existing dwelling units or by a change of occupancy:
1.1. The subdivided or newly created dwelling units shall be provided with 2 means
of egress.
1.2. Sprinklers shall be provided in accordance with Section 903 of the New York
City Building Code throughout the subdivided or newly created dwelling units, and
shall extend to protect the public hall and stair at the levels of such dwelling units.
1.3.Carbon monoxide and smoke alarms shall be provided throughout the
subdivided or newly created dwelling units, in accordance with Chapter 9 of the
New York City Building Code.
1.4. The building is not a frame building.
D602.2 Living room size. For buildings classified as HCA or HACA originally erected as a
one- or two-family dwelling on or before April 1, 1929, any requirement of this code that
covers the same subject matter as the following shall not apply, and such following
requirements shall apply in lieu thereof:
1. Every living room shall contain 550 cubic feet (16 m3) or more of air.
2. Every living room shall be at least 6 feet (1829 mm) wide at its narrowest part.
3. Every living room shall have a minimum height of 7 feet (2134 mm) if such room is in
the basement, of 7 feet (2134 mm) at all points more than 6 feet (1829mm) from the
front of such room if it is on the top story, and of 8 feet (2438 mm) if on any other
story.
D602.3 Natural light and ventilation. For buildings classified as HCA or HACA originally
erected as a one- or two -family dwelling on or before April 1, 1929, any requirement of this
code that covers the same subject matter as the following shall not apply, and such following
requirements shall apply in lieu thereof:
1. Every alcove, except a kitchenette or foyer, shall be deemed a living room and shall open
into an immediately adjoining living room which opens directly on a yard or street. Such
an alcove shall be separately lighted and ventilated, and of such size as provided for other
living rooms, unless it has an opening between the alcove and the room or space at least
80 percent of the area of the common wall and the floor area of the alcove does not exceed
twice the area of the opening. Alterations shall not increase the degree of noncompliance
for any existing unaltered alcoves.
2. Any room on a top story shall be permitted to be lighted and ventilated by an openable
skylight of the dimensions specified for windows and arranged to provide ventilating
openings of 6 square feet (0.5 m2) or more. Such a skylight shall be accepted in lieu of a
window.
D602.4. Means of Egress. Non-fireproof converted dwellings shall be permitted to retain their
existing means of egress, including where it is a single stair that is provided with sprinklers
and lawfully installed prior to the effective date of the local law that added this section. Where
any such single stair is to be relocated within the dwelling, such single means of egress shall
no longer be permitted to be retained, and a second means of egress shall be provided directly
accessible from within each dwelling unit and shall be either a system of outside fire-escapes,
constructed and arranged as provided in Section D306.3, a fire-stair, or a fire-tower. Where
such second means of egress discharges into a rear yard of less than 30 feet (9144 mm),
compliance with Section D303.6.12 shall be required.

EBC D603 Conversion to 3 families; no more than 3 stories. A building originally erected before

December 6, 1968, as a one- or two-family dwelling, 3 stories or less in height, shall be permitted
to be converted to a Class A multiple dwelling with no more than 3 families and shall be classified
EBC-CDA, provided such building meets this code and the following requirements. However, for
such building, any requirement of this code that covers the same subject matter as the following
shall not apply, and such following requirements in Sections D603.1 through D603.15 shall apply
in lieu thereof:
D603.1 Frame buildings. The building shall not be a frame building. However, the conversion
of nonresidential spaces in existing frame buildings to apartments shall be permitted in
accordance with the Exception to Section D306.5.
D603.2 Treads and risers. The treads and risers of every stair shall be of uniform height and
width in any 1 flight. Each tread, exclusive of nosing, shall be not less than 9 and one-half
inches wide (241.5 mm); each riser shall not exceed 7 ¾ inches (197 mm) in height; and the
product of the number of inches in the width of the tread and the number of inches in the height
of the riser shall be at least 70 inches (1778 mm) and at most 75 inches (1905 mm).
D603.3 Public hall. Every public stair hall shall provide access to the street as well as
extending to and through the roof by a bulkhead or scuttle, except that no bulkhead or scuttle,
or stair or ladder leading thereto, shall be required where the roof is a peak roof with a pitch of
more than 15 degrees (0.26 rad).
D603.4 Stairs. Existing wood stairs shall be permitted to remain. Existing stairs shall be at
least 30 inches (762 mm) in clear width.
D603.5 Entrance hall. The entrance hall to the street shall be at least 32 inches (813 mm) in
clear width.
D603.6 Scuttle. Any scuttle required by Section D603.3 shall be at least 21 inches (533 mm)
in width and 28 inches (711 mm) in length, covered on the outside with metal and provided
with a stair or a stationary iron ladder leading thereto and easily accessible to all occupants of
the dwelling. Every scuttle shall be located in the ceiling of the stair hall on the top story and
access to the roof through the scuttle shall be direct and uninterrupted. It shall be unlawful to
enclose in any manner the ladder or stair leading to a scuttle.
D603.7 Roof access. Every bulkhead door or scuttle shall not be self-locking and shall be
fastened on the inside with movable bolts, hooks, or a lock which does not require a key to
open from the inside of the dwelling.
D603.8 Business purposes. No part of such dwelling shall be used for business purposes,
except a home occupation as provided for in the New York City Zoning Resolution.
D603.9 Boarders, roomers, lodgers. No part of such dwelling shall be arranged, designed for,
or occupied by roomers, boarders, or lodgers.
D603.10 Dwelling unit doors. The dwelling unit entrance doors and door assemblies in public
stair halls and public halls shall be provided with self-closing door assemblies with a minimum
of a three-fourth-hour fire resistance rating.
D603.11 Fire rating of public hall and fire retarding of stairs. The public hall and stair
partitions shall be constructed as 1-hour fire barriers and the soffit of the stairs, if combustible,
shall be fire retarded in accordance with Section E402 of Appendix E.
D603.12 Fire rating of ceilings. The ceiling of the cellar and the ceiling of any dwelling unit
located below a different dwelling unit, or below a public hall or stair, shall be constructed as
horizontal assemblies with a fire-resistance rating of not less than 1 hour.
D603.13 Separation of dwelling units. Dwelling units shall be separated by assemblies
meeting a fire-resistance rating of not less than 1 hour.
D603.14 Cellar entrance. If there is a cellar, there shall be an independent entrance thereto
from outside the dwelling by means of a metal fire ladder or fireproof stair leading to an
opening in the outside cellar wall at least 30 inches (762 mm) in width and 6 feet (1829 mm)
in height. Such entrance to the cellar shall be permitted to be through an areaway or through a
fireproof passageway leading directly to the street. The entrance to the cellar may be closed
off with a grating or doors which shall not be locked or bolted unless they can be readily
unbolted or unlocked from the inside without a key and shall be arranged and constructed so
as to be readily opened at all times. Such cellar entrance shall not be used to satisfy a required
means of egress unless it also meets the standards required for such egress.
D603.15 Sprinklers. Sprinklers shall be provided throughout the building in accordance with
Section 903 of the New York City Building Code.

EBC D604 Conversion to more than 3 families and/or more than 3 stories. A building originally

erected as a one- or twof-family dwelling on or before April 1, 1929, shall be permitted to be
converted to a Class A multiple dwelling and shall be classified as ECB-CDA provided such
building meets this code and the following requirements. However, any requirement of this code
that covers the same subject matter as the following shall not apply, and such following
requirements from Sections D604.1 through D604.14 shall apply in lieu thereof:
D604.1 Frame buildings. The building shall not be a frame building. However, the conversion
of nonresidential spaces in existing frame buildings to apartments shall be converted in
accordance with the Exception to Section D306.5.
D604.2 Height and area limitations. The building, including any additions, shall comply with
the height and area limitations of Chapter 5 of the New York City Building Code, not to exceed
6 stories in height, as such term height is defined in the New York City Building Code.
D604.3 Fire alarm system. The building shall have a fire alarm system in accordance with
Section 907 of the New York City Building Code.
D604.4 Detection system. The building shall be provided with carbon monoxide and smoke
alarms in accordance with Chapter 9 of the New York City Building Code.
D604.5 Two means of egress. Egress from every dwelling unit on each story shall be provided
with 2 independent means of egress as follows:
D604.5.1 First means of egress. One means of egress shall be a stair providing access to
the street and comply with the following:
1. Roof access. The stair shall extend to and through the roof by a bulkhead, except
that a scuttle may be used in lieu of a bulkhead if the dwelling does not exceed in height
3 stories not including the basement, or if it does not exceed a basement and 4 other
stories in height and is occupied by not more than 2 families on any story, and except
that no bulkhead or scuttle, or stair or ladder leading thereto, shall be required where
the roof is a peak roof with a pitch of more than 15 degrees (0.26 rad).
2. Scuttles. Any scuttle provided pursuant to Section D604.5.1, Item 1, shall be at least
21 inches (533 mm) in width and 28 inches (711 mm) in length, covered on the outside
with metal and provided with a stair or a stationary iron ladder leading thereto and
easily accessible to all occupants of the dwelling. Every scuttle shall be located in the
ceiling of the stair hall on the top story and access to the roof through the scuttle shall
be direct and uninterrupted. It shall be unlawful to enclose in any manner the ladder or
stair leading to a scuttle.
3. Obstructions to roof access. Every bulkhead door or scuttle shall not be self-locking
and shall be fastened on the inside with movable bolts, hooks, or a lock which does not
require a key to open from the inside of the dwelling.
4. Existing wood stairs. Existing wood stairs shall be permitted to remain.
5. Independently enclosed public hall. Existing stairs without an intervening public
hall between the stair enclosure and the apartment entrance doors need not be provided.
6. Existing stair width. Existing stairs shall be at least 30 inches (762 mm) in clear
width for buildings up to 3 stories above a basement and 36 inches (914 mm) for all
other buildings. The entrance hall to the street shall be at least 32 inches (813 mm) in
clear width.
7. Wainscoting. Wood wainscoting shall be permitted to remain.
D604.5.2 Second means of egress. A second means of egress shall be directly accessible
from within each dwelling unit and shall be either a system of outside fire-escapes,
constructed and arranged as provided in Section D306.3, a fire-stair, or a fire-tower. Where
such second means of egress discharges into a rear yard of less than 30 feet (9144 mm),
compliance with Section D303.6.12 shall be required.
D604.6 Dwelling unit doors. The dwelling unit entrance doors and door assemblies in public
stair halls and public halls shall be provided with self-closing door assemblies with a minimum
of a ¾-hour fire resistance rating.
D604.7 Interior windows. All windows, transoms or other openings between dwelling units
and the public hall or stair hall shall be sealed with 1-hour fire-resistance-rated assemblies.
D604.8 Fire rating of public hall and fire retarding of stairs. The public hall and stair
partitions shall be constructed as 1-hour fire barriers and the soffit of the stairs, if combustible,
shall be fire retarded in accordance with Section E402 of Appendix E.
D604.9 Fire retarding of ceilings. The ceiling of the cellar, the ceiling of any dwelling unit
located below a different dwelling unit or below a public hall or stair and the ceiling of a
commercial space shall be constructed as horizontal assemblies with a fire-resistance rating of
not less than 1 hour.
D604.10 Separation of dwelling units. Dwelling units shall be separated by assemblies
meeting a fire-resistance rating of not less than 1 hour.
D604.11 Living room size. Every living room:
1. Shall contain 550 cubic feet (16 m3) or more of air;
2. Shall be at least 6 feet (1829 mm) wide at its narrowest part;
3. Shall have a minimum height of 7 feet (2134 mm) if such room is in the basement, of 7
feet (2134 mm) at all points more than 6 feet (1829 mm) from the front of such room if it
is on the top story, and of 8 feet (2438 mm) if on any other story.
D604.12 Natural light and ventilation. The natural light and ventilation shall comply with
the following:
1. Every alcove, except a kitchenette or foyer, shall be deemed a living room and shall open
into an immediately adjoining living room which opens directly on a yard or street. Such
an alcove shall be separately lighted and ventilated, and of such size as provided for other
living rooms, unless it has an opening between the alcove and the room or space at least
80 percent of the area of the common wall and the floor area of the alcove does not exceed
twice the area of the opening.
2. Any room on a top story shall be permitted to be lighted and ventilated by a skylight of
the dimensions specified for windows and arranged to provide ventilating openings of 6
square feet (0.5 m2) or more. Such a skylight shall be accepted in lieu of a window.
D604.13 Cellar entrance. If there is a cellar, there shall be an independent entrance thereto
from outside the dwelling by means of a metal fire ladder or fireproof stair leading to an
opening in the outside cellar wall at least 30 inches (762 mm) in width and 6 feet (1829 mm)
in height. Such entrance to the cellar shall be permitted to be through an areaway or through a
fireproof passageway leading directly to the street. The entrance to the cellar may be closed
off with a grating or doors which shall not be locked or bolted unless they can be readily
unbolted or unlocked from the inside without a key and shall be arranged and constructed so
as to be readily opened at all times. Such cellar entrance shall not be used to satisfy a required
means of egress unless it also meets the standards required for such egress.
D604.14 Sprinklers. Sprinklers shall be provided throughout the building in accordance with
Section 903 of the New York City Building Code.
D604.15 Elevator. The building shall be required to provide an elevator where required by
Section D306.1.

EBC D605 Rooms in basements and cellars

D605.1 New rooms in basements or cellars (MDL 34(6), 177(1)). Rooms created after the
effective date of the local law that added this section in basements and cellars within dwelling
units in converted dwellings shall comply with the requirements of Section D305.4.1.
D605.2 Existing rooms in basements and cellars (MDL 34(6), 177(5)). For the purposes of
establishing the lawful conditions of existing rooms in basements or cellars within dwelling
units in converted dwellings, refer to Section D305.4.1 or Sections 177(1) to (4) of the MDL.

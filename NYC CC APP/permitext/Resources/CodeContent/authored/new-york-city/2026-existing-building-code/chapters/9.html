CHAPTER 9

ALTERATIONS—LEVEL 2

Enacted, not yet effective. Effective July 17, 2027.

EBC 901 GENERAL

901.1 Scope. Level 2 alterations as described in Section 603 shall comply with the requirements
of this chapter and Chapter 3.
901.2 Compliance. In addition to the provisions of this chapter, level 2 alteration work shall
comply with all of the requirements of Chapter 8. The requirements of Sections 803, 804 and 805
shall apply to Level 2 alteration work within all work areas whether or not they include exits and
corridors shared by more than 1 tenant and regardless of the occupant load, except as otherwise
provided in Sections 903, 904, and 905.
901.2.1 Building safety performance and scoring method. The design professional of record
may elect to comply with Chapter 13 as an alternative to compliance with any of the
requirements of Sections 903, 904 and 905, including Sections 803, 804 and 805, in accordance
with Section 301.1.4.
901.2.2 Work that increases floor surface area of an existing building by more than 110
percent. Where a Level 2 alteration made to an existing building causes the existing floor
surface area to be increased by more than 110 percent pursuant to Section 302.7, such building
shall comply with the New York City Building Code as if hereafter erected.

EBC 902 SPECIAL USE AND OCCUPANCY

902.1 Uses and occupancies subject to Chapter 4 of the New York City Building Code. Except
as stated in Section 901.2.2 of this code, where an existing building occupied by a use or occupancy
that is identified in Chapter 4 of the New York City Building Code is undergoing a level 2 alteration,
such alteration work within the work area shall comply with the applicable requirements of
Chapter 4 of the New York City Building Code and this section.
902.1.1 High-rise buildings. Within work areas in buildings having occupied floors more than
75 feet (22 860 mm) above the lowest level of fire department vehicle access, alteration work
shall comply with applicable provisions of Section 403 of the New York City Building Code
and Sections 902.1.1.1 through 902.1.1.7 of this code.
902.1.1.1 Smoke detection systems control and ducts and air transfer openings. All
duct and air transfer openings within the work area and all fan systems serving the work
area shall be newly installed or made to comply with Sections 606 and 607 of the New York
City Mechanical Code.
Exception: The standby power requirements of Sections 606 and 607 of the New York
City Mechanical Code shall not apply if both of the following conditions are met:
1. Standby power systems do not exist or existing standby power systems have
insufficient capacity to serve this load.
2. New standby or emergency power is not required by Section 902.1.1.7 of this
code.
902.1.1.2 Elevators. Where there is an elevator or elevators for public use, at least 1
elevator serving the work area shall comply with this section. Existing elevators with a
travel distance of 25 feet (7620 mm) or more above or below the main floor or other level
of a building and intended to serve the needs of emergency personnel for fire-fighting or
rescue purposes shall be provided with emergency operation in accordance with ASME
A17.3 as amended by Appendix K of the New York City Building Code. New elevators
shall be provided with Phase I emergency recall operation and Phase II emergency in-car
operation in accordance with ASME A17.1/CSAB44.1 as amended by Appendix K of the
New York City Building Code.
902.1.1.3 Additional exit stairway. In existing buildings erected pursuant to the laws in
effect prior to December 31, 2014, with a height exceeding 420 feet (128 m), where the
work area exceeds 75 percent of the building area, the requirements for additional exit
stairway of Section 403.5.2 of the New York City Building Code shall apply.
Exceptions:
1. In buildings where at least 50 percent of passenger elevators intended for
general public use, including other elevators serving all floors, are used for
occupant self-evacuation in compliance with Sections 3008.1 through 3008.11
of the New York City Building Code.
2. Existing fully sprinklered buildings shall be considered in compliance with
Section 3008.3.2 of the New York City Building Code.
902.1.1.4 Structural integrity of exit enclosures and elevator hoistway enclosures.
Where the work area includes stairs or elevator hoistway enclosures, the design
professional of record shall determine compliance of the enclosures with the structural
integrity provisions of Section 403.2.3 of the New York City Building Code. Where the
existing wall assembly of such enclosures do not meet the requirements of Section 403.2.3
of the New York City Building Code, the occupied side of such wall assembly within the
work area shall comply with 1 of the methods provided in Section 403.2.3.2. In prior code
buildings, existing concrete, masonry or masonry equivalent exit and elevator hoistway
enclosures shall be deemed to comply with the structural integrity requirements of Section
403.2.3 of the New York City Building Code.
Exception: New, relocated or altered exit and elevator hoistway enclosures shall
comply with all of the requirements of Section 403.2.3 of the New York City Building
Code.
902.1.1.5 Smokeproof exit enclosures. In prior code buildings, where the work area
exceeds 75 percent of the building area, smokeproof exit enclosures shall be provided for
all exit stairways throughout the building in accordance with Sections 403.5.4, 909.20 and
1023.11 of the New York City Building Code.
902.1.1.6 Elevator lobbies in prior code buildings. Elevator lobbies required by Section
403.6.3 shall be constructed in accordance with Section 3006.1.1 or Section 3006.1.2 of
the New York City Building Code, as applicable, on each floor being altered where the work
area exceeds 50 percent of that floor.
Exceptions.
1. Elevator lobbies shall not be required for elevators that are located within other
occupied tenant spaces that are not subject to alteration work.
2. Notwithstanding Exception 1, where the work area exceeds 75 percent of the
building area, elevator lobbies shall be constructed in accordance with Section
3006.1.1 or Section 3006.1.2 of the New York City Building Code, as applicable,
on all floors served by elevators.
902.1.1.6.1 Access to an exit stair not available. Except as permitted by Section
902.1.1.6.2, where the elevator lobby cannot provide access to an exit, a red telephone
shall be installed in the vestibule near the elevator doors in the elevator lobby to
communicate with the main lobby fire command station or building manager’s office
or to a central service station when the building is not attended. The phone shall provide
two-way communication in accordance with Section 907.2.13.3 of the New York City
Building Code and shall comply with the requirements of NFPA 72 as modified by
Appendix Q of the New York City Building Code. A sign shall be posted near the phone.
The sign shall read “In Case of Fire or Other Emergency, Use This Phone to Contact
Lobby or Building Manager or Central Service Station”.
902.1.1.6.2 Locking of lobby doors. Where locking devices are proposed on elevator
lobby doors for security reasons to prevent access that leads to an exit, such locking
devices shall be installed in accordance with Appendix K of the New York City Building
Code.
902.1.1.7 Emergency and standby systems. Where an existing building is undergoing a
Level 2 alteration and the work area exceeds 75 percent of the building area, the building
shall comply with Chapter 27 of the New York City Building Code.
902.1.2 Boiler and furnace equipment rooms. Boiler and furnace equipment rooms located
within single occupancy or mixed occupancy existing buildings of Occupancy Groups I-1, I-
2, I-4, R-1 or R-2 shall be enclosed by fire-resistance-rated construction in accordance with
Section 509 of the New York City Building Code.

EBC 903 BUILDING ELEMENTS AND MATERIALS

903.1 Existing shafts and vertical openings. Existing shafts and vertical openings, including
stairways, shall be enclosed and protected in accordance with the New York City Building Code
from the highest level within the work area, including the level of exit discharge and all floors
below the level of exit discharge. Where the proposed alteration results in a reduction in the
existing fire-resistive rating, the registered design professional of record shall demonstrate
compliance with Section 303.2 of this code.
903.1.1 Prior code buildings. In prior code buildings, existing shafts and vertical openings,
including stairways, shall be enclosed and protected in accordance with Section 803.2.1 from
the highest level within the work area including the level of exit discharge and all floors below.
903.2 Fire separations in Group R-3. Fire separation in Group R-3 occupancies shall be in
accordance with Section 903.2.1.
903.2.1 Separation required in R-3 occupancy. Where the work area in any attached one- or
two-family dwellings results in the removal of wall or ceiling finishes exposing the existing
fire separation that was constructed in accordance with Appendix M of the New York City
Building Code or department issued memorandum on the same subject matter, special
inspection shall be performed in accordance with Section 1705.17 of the New York City
Building Code on the side of the dwelling unit wall that is part of the work area to determine
whether the assembly is in compliance with Chapter 7 of the New York City Building Code.
Any wall or portions thereof separating the dwelling units that are identified as not continuous
from the foundation to the underside of the roof sheathing shall be constructed to provide a
continuous fire separation using construction materials consistent with the existing wall or
complying with the requirements for new structures. Such work shall be limited to the side of
the dwelling unit wall that is part of the work area.
903.3 Interior finish. The interior finish of walls and ceilings in exits and corridors in any work
area or exit serving the work area, between the highest floor on which there is a work area and the
floor of exit discharge, shall comply with the requirements of Section 803.4.
Exception: Interior finish of walls and ceilings in corridors and exits within tenant spaces that
are entirely outside the work area.
903.4 Protection of window openings on lot line air shafts in Group R-2 buildings. Where a
prior code building is undergoing a Level 2 alteration and the building contains windows that open
onto a lot line air shaft as defined in Chapter 2, and any part of such window is less than 3 feet
(914 mm) from the lot line or less than 6 feet (1829 mm) from another window on the adjacent
building, all such windows shall be protected in accordance with Section 803.8. For the purpose
of this section, separation distance to adjacent buildings shall be measured in any direction to the
nearest edges of window openings.
Exception. Windows located in occupied tenant spaces outside the work area.
903.4.1 Work areas exceeding 75 percent of the building area. Where the work area exceeds
75 percent of building area, all windows opening on lot line air shafts shall be protected in
accordance with Section 803.8 throughout the building.
903.5 Existing buildings in fire districts. Alterations made to existing buildings shall comply
with Sections 302.6 and 803.9.

EBC 904 FIRE PROTECTION

904.1 General. Existing buildings undergoing a Level 2 alteration shall be provided with fire
protection systems in accordance with this section, Section 304 and Section 804, as applicable.
904.1.1 Compliance within the work area. See Sections 904.2 and 904.4.
904.1.2 Compliance throughout the building. The entire building shall comply with Chapter
9 of the New York City Building Code as if hereafter erected where:
1. The Level 2 alteration work is covered by Section 901.2.2 of this code; or
2. The Level 2 alteration work area exceeds 75 percent of the building area.
904.2 Automatic sprinkler systems. Except as stated in Sections 904.2.1 through 904.2.3, an
automatic sprinkler system shall be provided in the work area in accordance with Section 804.2
regardless of the occupant load, the percentage of the work area per floor, and whether or not the
work area includes exits or corridors serving more than 1 tenant. The design and installation of the
system shall be in accordance with Chapter 9 of the New York City Building Code, except as
permitted for prior code buildings in Section 804.2.8 of this code.
904.2.1 High-rise buildings. Where a prior code high-rise building is undergoing a Level 2
alteration, an automatic sprinkler system shall be provided in work areas as required by Chapter
9 of the New York City Building Code. Prior code buildings with existing sprinkler systems
may use the provisions of Section 804.2.8 of this code.
904.2.2 Rubbish and linen chutes. Rubbish and linen chutes located in the work area shall be
provided with an automatic sprinkler system where protection of the rubbish and linen chute
would be required under the provisions of the New York City Building Code for new
construction.
904.2.3 Upholstered furniture or mattresses. Work areas shall be provided with an automatic
sprinkler system in accordance with the New York City Building Code where any of the
following conditions exist within the work area:
1. A Group F-1 occupancy used for the manufacture of upholstered furniture or mattresses
exceeds 2,500 square feet (232 m2).
2. A Group M occupancy used for the display and sale of upholstered furniture or
mattresses exceeds 5,000 square feet (464 m2).
3. A Group S-1 occupancy used for the storage of upholstered furniture or mattresses
exceeds 2,500 square feet (232 m2).
904.3 Reserved.
904.4 Fire alarm and detection systems. Fire alarm and detection systems shall be provided
throughout the work area in accordance with Section 907 of the New York City Building Code as
required for new construction.

EBC 905 MEANS OF EGRESS

905.1 General. Except as stated in Section 901.2.2, the means of egress shall comply with the
requirements of Sections 305 and 805 within the work area that includes exits and corridors
regardless of the number of tenants served by such exits and corridors. The means of egress shall
also comply with Sections 905.1.1 and 905.1.2.
905.1.1 Means-of-egress lighting. All interior exit stairways and ramps from the highest work
area floor to the exit discharge shall be provided with artificial lighting in accordance with the
requirements of Chapter 10 of the New York City Building Code.
905.1.2 Exit signs. Means of egress from the highest work area floor to the exit discharge shall
be provided with exit signs in accordance with the requirements of Chapter 10 of the New York
City Building Code.
905.2 Existing combustible stairs in prior code buildings. Where a prior code building of
Occupancy Group R-1 or R-2 is undergoing a Level 2 alteration and the work area exceeds 75
percent of the building area, existing exit stairs constructed of combustible materials shall be
replaced in their entirety with non-combustible construction, including stairs, landings, handrails
and guards. The replacement stair shall meet the requirements of Chapter 10 of the New York City
Building Code. Where the size of the existing shaft does not allow for full compliance with Chapter
10 of the New York City Building Code in regards to required dimensions, the replacement stair
may comply with Section 805.12.1 of this code.
Exception: Prior code buildings that are fully sprinklered and comply with the fire protection
requirements of Chapter 9 of the New York City Building Code.

EBC 906 ACCESSIBILITY

906.1 General. Within the work area, a building, facility or element that is altered shall comply
with Chapter 11 of the New York City Building Code, this section and Sections 306 and 806 of this
code.
Exception: Where the commissioner grants a waiver from certain requirements of Chapter 11
of the New York City Building Code pursuant to Section 306 of this code.
906.1.1 Applicability of Chapter 11 of the New York City Building Code to the entire
building. Where a building is undergoing a Level 2 alteration, the entire building shall comply
with the provisions of Chapter 11 of the New York City Building Code as if hereafter erected.
Exception: In a building of Occupancy Group R-2, where the dwelling or sleeping units
included in the work area exceed more than 50 percent of the dwelling or sleeping units in
the building and are under a single ownership, all common use facilities serving those
dwelling or sleeping units shall comply with the requirements of Chapter 11 of the New
York City Building Code.

EBC 907 STRUCTURAL

907.1 General. Where existing buildings are undergoing Level 2 alterations including structural
alterations, the provisions of Chapter 7 shall apply.
907.2. Structural condition assessment. Level 2 alterations shall require an initial structural
condition assessment pursuant to Section 704.3. Where the Level 2 alteration includes a structural
alteration, a detailed structural condition assessment pursuant to Section 704.4 is also required.

EBC 908 ELECTRICAL

908.1 General. All electrical equipment and wiring within work areas shall comply with all
applicable requirements of the New York City Electrical Code, this section and Sections 308 and
808 of this code.
908.1.1 Compliance of the entire electrical system as new construction. Where the building
is undergoing a Level 2 alteration and the work area exceeds 75 percent of the building area,
the entire electrical system shall comply with the provisions of the New York City Electrical
Code for new construction.

EBC 909 MECHANICAL

909.1 General. Mechanical elements and systems within work areas in buildings undergoing a
Level 2 alteration shall comply with Section 309, Section 809 and this section.
909.2 Applicability of New York City Mechanical Code. Where an existing building is
undergoing a Level 2 alteration, all existing mechanical systems within the work area, including
components, equipment and appliances, shall comply with the New York City Mechanical Code,
this section and Section 809.2 of this code.
909.2.1 Ventilation. Ventilation systems serving work areas of a Level 2 alteration shall be
newly installed or altered to comply with requirements of Chapter 4 of the New York City
Mechanical Code for new construction.
909.2.2 Exhaust systems. Exhaust systems serving work areas of a Level 2 alteration shall be
newly installed or altered to comply with requirements of Chapter 5 of the New York City
Mechanical Code for new construction.
909.2.3 Compliance of ventilation, exhaust, and duct systems as new construction. Where
the building is undergoing a Level 2 alteration and the work area exceeds 75 percent of the
building floor area, all ventilation, exhaust and duct systems shall be newly installed or altered
to comply with the provisions of Chapters 4, 5 and 6 of the New York City Mechanical Code
for new construction.

EBC 910 PLUMBING

910.1 General. Plumbing elements and systems within work areas in buildings undergoing a Level
2 alteration shall comply with Section 310, Section 810 and this section.
910.1.1 Compliance of the entire plumbing system as new construction. Where the building
is undergoing a Level 2 alteration and the work area exceeds 75 percent of the building area,
the entire plumbing system shall comply with the provisions of the New York City Plumbing
Code for new construction.
Exception. Plumbing system components that are found to be in code-compliant condition
upon inspection and certification by a licensed master plumber shall be permitted to
remain.

EBC 911 FUEL GAS

911.1 General. Fuel gas elements and systems within work areas in buildings undergoing a Level
2 alteration shall comply with Section 311, Section 811, and this section.
911.1.1 Compliance of entire fuel gas system as new construction. Where the building is
undergoing a Level 2 alteration and the work area exceeds 75 percent of the building floor
area, the entire fuel gas system shall comply with the provisions of the New York City Fuel
Gas Code for new construction.
Exception. Fuel gas system components that are found to be in code-compliant condition
upon inspection and certification by a licensed master plumber shall be permitted to
remain.

EBC 912 ENERGY CONSERVATION

912.1 Minimum requirements. Level 2 alterations to existing buildings or structures are
permitted without requiring the entire building or structure to comply with the energy requirements
of the New York City Energy Conservation Code. Level 2 alterations shall conform to the energy
requirements of Chapters R5 or C5 of the New York City Energy Conservation Code, as applicable.

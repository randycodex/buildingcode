APPENDIX S

SUPPLEMENTARY FIGURES FOR LUMINOUS EGRESS PATH MARKINGS

Historical edition. Applicability depends on the filing and project facts.

BC S101.1 Scope.

The figures of this appendix shall supplement the provisions of luminous egress path markings in Section 1024
and are intended for illustrative purposes. Where there is a conflict between the figures and the provisions in Section 1024, the
provisions in Section 1024 shall govern.

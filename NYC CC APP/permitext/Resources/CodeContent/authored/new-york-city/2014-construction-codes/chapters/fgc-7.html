CHAPTER 7

GASEOUS HYDROGEN SYSTEMS

Historical edition. Applicability depends on the filing and project facts.

FGC 701.1 Scope.

The installation of gaseous hydrogen systems shall comply with this chapter and Chapters 30 and 35 of the New York City Fire Code. Compressed gases shall also comply with Chapter 27 of the New York City Fire Code for general requirements.

FGC 701.2 Permits.

Permits shall be required as set forth in Section 105 of this code and as required by the New York City Fire Code.

Last amended 2024-06-06

(6/6/24)

The boundaries of certain designated areas within Manufacturing Districts are shown on the maps in this APPENDIX. Designated areas in which self-service storage facilities are subject to the as-of-right provisions of Section 42-19 (Use Group IX – Storage)  are shown on the maps in Subarea 1, and those in which such uses are subject to special permit of the City Planning Commission pursuant to Section 74-192 (Self-service storage facility in designated areas within Manufacturing Districts) are shown on the maps in Subarea 2.

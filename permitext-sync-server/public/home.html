BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few details.

Which codes can I browse?

The web library includes the 2022 construction codes, 2014 historical editions, Fire Code, Housing Maintenance Code, zoning, and other NYC code collections. Choose a code and edition in Reader; historical and future-effective material is labeled separately.

Do I need an account?

You can browse, read, and search without signing in. Saving and Research require Pro. Account and plan details are explained in the workspace.

A NOTE ON SOURCES

Check the source.

Check the code, edition, and source information for each provision. Official sources and applicable amendments govern.

Permitext is an independent research tool. It does not provide agency approval or an official determination.

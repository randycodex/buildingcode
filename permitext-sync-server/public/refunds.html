Subscription and Refund Policy

Prepared August 22, 2026

Beta 1 working draft. The proposed seven-day web refund rule below needs owner approval.

Subscription terms

Permitext Pro is $20 per month with no free trial. It renews automatically each month until canceled. Code reading and search remain available on the Free plan after Pro ends.

Canceling an Apple subscription

Apple bills purchases made in the iOS app. Cancel through the subscription settings for the Apple Account that made the purchase. Permitext cannot cancel an Apple subscription for you. Cancellation normally prevents the next renewal while access continues through the paid period. Apple cancellation instructions explain the current steps.

Apple refund requests

Apple controls eligibility and processing for App Store refunds. Submit an eligible request at reportaproblem.apple.com. Permitext cannot approve, issue, or redirect an Apple refund. If Apple revokes or refunds the transaction, Permitext will remove the related Pro entitlement when the provider event is received.

Canceling a web subscription

Stripe bills purchases made on the Permitext website. A signed-in subscriber can use Manage Subscription in Settings to open Stripe's billing portal and cancel. Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current paid period unless the portal clearly states otherwise. Keep the cancellation confirmation for your records.

Proposed web refund rule

For the initial web subscription charge, Permitext will provide a full refund when the request is emailed to permitext@gmail.com within seven calendar days of the charge and the account has used no more than five paid Research turns. This limited first-charge policy is intended to let a new subscriber correct an unsuitable purchase without transferring substantial Research cost to other subscribers.

Renewal charges and partially used billing periods are otherwise nonrefundable, except where required by law or when Permitext confirms a duplicate charge, unauthorized charge, or material technical failure that prevented delivery of the paid service. Canceling does not itself create a refund. Approved Stripe refunds return to the original payment method; bank processing time can vary.

As an additional proposed safeguard, an accidental web renewal will receive a full refund when requested within 72 hours of the renewal charge and the account has used no more than five paid Research turns since that renewal. After that window, the general nonrefundable rule applies.

Duplicate Apple and web subscriptions

One Permitext account needs only one active Pro subscription. If Apple and Stripe both bill the same person, cancel the subscription you do not want to renew and contact support. Apple must decide any refund for the Apple charge; Permitext may review the Stripe charge under the web policy above.

Lifetime access grants

Promotional or owner-issued Lifetime Pro access is not a paid subscription, has no cash value, is nontransferable, and may be revoked by Permitext. Revocation of a grant does not affect a separately purchased Apple or Stripe subscription.

How to request help

Email permitext@gmail.com from the address associated with the Permitext account. Include the purchase platform, approximate charge date, and the problem. Do not email card numbers, passwords, provider keys, or authentication codes.

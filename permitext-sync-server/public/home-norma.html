A few details.

Who is Permitext for?

Architects, engineers, and people who need to read and research NYC codes.

Do I need an account?

You can browse, read, and search without signing in. Saving and Research require Pro. Account and plan details are explained in the workspace.

What should I check when using a provision?

Check its code, edition, and source information. Official sources and applicable amendments govern. Permitext is an independent research tool and does not provide an official determination.

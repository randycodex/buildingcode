### acceptance-water-heater-valve

**Turn 1:** Explain the current 2022 NYC Plumbing Code cold-water branch valve requirements for a water heater: what it serves, its location and access.

Bounded conclusion: The cold-water branch from the main supply to each hot-water storage tank or water heater must have a valve located near the equipment. The valve must serve only that tank or heater, must not interfere with or disrupt the remainder of the cold-water system, and must be accessible on the same floor level as the heater served.

- A valve is required on the cold-water branch from the main supply to each hot-water storage tank or water heater. [PC 503.1]
- The valve must be located near the equipment. [PC 503.1]
- The valve must serve only the hot-water storage tank or water heater and must not interfere with or disrupt the remainder of the cold-water system. [PC 503.1]
- The valve must have access on the same floor level as the water heater served. [PC 503.1]

Forbidden claims:

- Do not replace the requirement that the valve serve only the tank or heater with a mere proximity test.
- Do not assert a specific distance for “near” or define access beyond the supplied text.

Required uncertainty:

- The provision does not quantify “near” or further define access; a particular installation’s satisfaction of those terms cannot be determined without relevant project facts or applicable interpretive authority.

**Turn 2:** In this hypothetical, the valve also shuts off the cold-water supply to another fixture. Is proximity to the heater enough to satisfy this valve rule?

Bounded conclusion: No. A valve that also shuts off another fixture does not serve only the water heater, so proximity to the heater is not enough to satisfy PC 503.1. The rule separately prohibits interference with or disruption of the remainder of the cold-water system.

- The valve must serve only the hot-water storage tank or water heater; serving another fixture fails that condition. [PC 503.1]
- Near-equipment location does not replace the separate serve-only condition. [PC 503.1]
- The valve must not interfere with or disrupt the remainder of the cold-water system. [PC 503.1]

Forbidden claims:

### Permitext answer

No. Based on the stated 55 percent gross-floor-area figure, the requested three-floor partial certificate cannot be issued under AC 28-118.20: the floors covered by a partial certificate may not constitute more than 50 percent of the building’s gross floor area. The owner’s statements about noncombustible construction, sprinklers, and egress cannot cure that separate percentage limit.

Even if the request were reduced or the verified calculation showed no more than 50 percent, issuance remains conditional on establishing all of the following: the existing building was erected before January 1, 1938; it neither has nor is otherwise required to have a certificate of occupancy or, if applicable, certificate of completion; the building—not merely the three requested floors—is of noncombustible construction and protected by an automatic sprinkler system; adequate means of egress are provided from all floors; and, upon inspection, the building is deemed safe for occupancy. The owner’s representations do not themselves establish these conditions.

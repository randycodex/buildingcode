The owner’s claim is not supported by these passages. If the proposed café is beyond the building line, it must comply not only with this Building Code section but also with the Zoning Resolution, the requirements of the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection and Department of Transportation commissioners, and Chapter 32 projection limits. An outdoor-dining authorization therefore does not, on this evidence, eliminate those separate compliance obligations.

The selected obstruction rule applies to a sidewalk café’s awning, enclosure, fixture, equipment, or removable platform: none may obstruct a building exit or a cellar access hatch or areaway. The supplied passages do not state whether the proposed furniture is a  fixture  or  equipment,  so they do not by themselves determine whether this particular furniture is covered by that rule. If a platform is proposed over the hatch, the only supplied exception is conditional: upon special application, the commissioner may permit an easily removable, prominently designated platform designed under Section 3111.5, but only where the cellar entrance or areaway is not used as a required means of egress. The facts provided do not establish that exception.

Accessibility review is not eliminated. The sidewalk café and access to it must comply with Chapter 11. Where dining surfaces are provided, at least 10 percent of the total seating and standing spaces must be accessible, with at least one accessible dining surface of each type; those surfaces must be distributed throughout the facility and be on a level accessed by an accessible route. Compliance cannot be determined from the available seating/standing counts, dining-surface types, layout, distribution, and route information.

Separate exterior seating cannot yet be accepted for the limited assembly-seating determination addressed here. For an enclosed sidewalk café, its seating must be added to interior seating to determine whether a place-of-assembly certificate of operation is required, unless it is separated from the interior seating by fire partitions complying with Section 713. Whether this café is enclosed—and, if it is, whether a compliant fire partition exists—is unresolved. The supplied text does not create a general rule that all exterior seating can be counted separately, and it does not itself establish whether a certificate is required.

The material unresolved record is the café location relative to the building line; plans showing the exit, hatch/areaway, and each proposed awning, enclosure, fixture, equipment item, or platform; whether the furniture qualifies as a covered fixture or equipment; whether the hatch/areaway is a required means of egress; any special application and commissioner permission for a platform; enclosure and fire-partition details; seating and standing totals, dining-surface types, accessible-surface locations, and accessible route; and the applicable current agency authorizations and requirements.

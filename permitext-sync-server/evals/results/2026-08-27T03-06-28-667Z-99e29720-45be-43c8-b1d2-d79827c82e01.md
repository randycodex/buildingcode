### Permitext answer

Not if the room is both used for assembly purposes and accessory to the apartment-building occupancy. At the stated tables-and-chairs arrangement, the occupant-load factor is 15 net square feet per person; 900 net square feet ÷ 15 = 60 occupants. Because 60 is fewer than 75, BC 303.1.3 requires the room to be classified as Group B or as part of the accessory occupancy—not as Group A-3. The exception preserves the option to calculate that room’s plumbing fixtures under the assembly-occupancy requirements.

This conclusion depends on the room legally being accessory to the other occupancy. Residents-only use supports the described relationship as a project fact, but the supplied code text does not itself define or establish accessory status. If it is not accessory, the supplied evidence does not resolve its final occupancy classification.

Fixture calculations in Group B office occupancies shall utilize the total occupant load on a given floor to determine the number of fixtures required for that floor.
> Exception: The total occupant load shall not be required to be divided in half where approved statistical data indicates a distribution of the sexes of other than 50 percent of each sex.

### Permitext answer

Not automatically. If the room actually qualifies as an accessory assembly space with an occupant load below 75, BC 303.1.3 classifies it as Group B (or part of its accessory occupancy) but expressly permits its plumbing-fixture count to be calculated under the Assembly occupancy requirements. The supplied evidence does not establish that the normal Group B fixture calculation is also permitted for that accessory room merely because of that classification.

PC 403.1 generally bases fixtures on the occupancy classification and Table 403.1, but that table was not supplied. The separate PC 403.1 Assembly-calculation permission you provided applies to a building or nonaccessory tenant space; it is therefore not independent authority for this accessory multipurpose room. For this room, the directly applicable supplied provision is BC 303.1.3's Assembly-calculation option.

No fixture count can be calculated from the supplied passages because the applicable Table 403.1 fixture ratios and the room's final occupant load/use category are not established. If a count is made under the table, the default is to divide occupant load equally by sex, apply the applicable ratios, add fractional requirements across occupancies before rounding up, and round up to whole fixtures. A different sex distribution requires approved statistical data.

- Fixture requirements ordinarily follow the occupancy table: Plumbing fixtures must be provided for the occupancy type and in the minimum number in Table 403.1; occupant load and occupancy classification are determined under the Building Code. The supplied evidence does not include the Table 403.1 Group B or Assembly rows, so it does not establish either numerical fixture result or an affirmative normal-Group-B calculation option for this accessory room.

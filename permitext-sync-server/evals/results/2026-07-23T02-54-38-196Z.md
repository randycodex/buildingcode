### Permitext answer

**Conclusion:** Yes—conditionally. For a Group R-2 occupancy, two stairs in a scissor stair assembly may be treated as two separate exit stairways if the stated R-2 exception conditions are met: (1) the exit doors are at least 15 feet apart, and (2) the stairs and the construction separating them have the specified 2-hour, masonry-or-masonry-equivalent construction. Because the doors are stated to be 15 feet apart, the door-separation criterion is met if the measurement is taken as required by the code. [§1007.1.1, Passage d157ae75-1100-449b-a889-4011bdf75c13]

The general rule says that stairs sharing a common wall, floor, ceiling, scissor stair assembly, or other enclosure are counted as one exit stairway. [§1007.1.1, Passage 84624b04-cc11-4a07-a343-9e53060e0b96] However, the selected Group R-2 provision expressly permits stairs to share scissor stair assemblies or other enclosures when the required 2-hour masonry or masonry-equivalent construction conditions are satisfied, and requires the exit doors to be no less than 15 feet apart. [§1007.1.1, Passage d157ae75-1100-449b-a889-4011bdf75c13] Thus, 15 feet is sufficient for the stated door-spacing condition, but spacing alone does not establish that the two stairs can be counted separately.

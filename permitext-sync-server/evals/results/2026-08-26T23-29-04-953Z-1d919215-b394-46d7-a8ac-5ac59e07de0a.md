### Permitext answer

No. This NYC Building Code section does not prove that HCR requires a bathroom vanity—or even that a lavatory must be provided. It applies only where the water closet has only a forward approach. In that circumstance, it sets water-closet clearance dimensions and says a compliant lavatory is *permitted* on the rear wall if it is at least 18 inches from the water-closet centerline.  Permitted  authorizes that location; it does not mandate the fixture.

If a lavatory is provided under this provision, it must comply with the referenced lavatory rule. That rule permits removable cabinetry below the lavatory under stated conditions, but likewise does not require cabinetry or a vanity.

The supplied evidence does not identify what  HCR  means or provide an HCR program, standard, or rule. Provide the exact HCR authority and the cited requirement if the question is whether that separate authority mandates a vanity.

**What the selected evidence establishes**

- The cited section permits rather than requires a lavatory: Where only a forward approach is provided to a water closet, the section requires 66 inches of rear-wall clearance and 48 inches of side-wall clearance. It then states that a compliant lavatory is permitted on the rear wall at least 18 inches from the water-closet centerline. This is a conditional clearance rule and location permission, not a mandate to install a lavatory or vanity.
- The referenced lavatory rule regulates an installed lavatory: The referenced section requires lavatories to comply with ICC A117.1 Section 606 and permits cabinetry under a lavatory only if it is removable without removing or replacing the lavatory, the finish floor continues below it, and the surrounding walls are finished. It does not state that a lavatory, cabinetry, or vanity must be provided.

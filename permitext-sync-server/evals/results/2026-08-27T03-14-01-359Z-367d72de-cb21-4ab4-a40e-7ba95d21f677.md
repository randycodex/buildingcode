### Permitext answer

No. This NYC Building Code section does not prove that HCR requires a vanity.

It addresses water-closet clearance where only a forward approach is provided. It permits a compliant **lavatory** on the rear wall if it is at least 18 inches from the water-closet centerline; it does not require a lavatory, and it says nothing about a **vanity**. A lavatory and a vanity are distinct terms, so the permission for a lavatory cannot establish a vanity requirement.

The broader supplied NYC provision applies only where toilet or bathing rooms are provided in a Type B+NYC dwelling or sleeping unit, and in that scope it requires at least one compliant lavatory and water closet (and, in a bathing room, a bathtub or shower). That still does not establish a vanity requirement or an HCR requirement. To evaluate an asserted HCR requirement, the applicable HCR design standard, funding/program requirement, or official HCR guidance is needed.

The broader supplied NYC provision does require at least one lavatory in each toilet or bathing room within a Type B+NYC dwelling or sleeping unit, subject to its stated Type A exception. The supplied text uses lavatory, not vanity”; it does not establish whether a particular vanity cabinet, countertop, or configuration is required.

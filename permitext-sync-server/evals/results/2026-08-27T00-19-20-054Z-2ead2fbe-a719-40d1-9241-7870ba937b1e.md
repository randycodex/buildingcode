Yes—potentially, but not based on the 15-foot door spacing alone. The general rule counts stairs that share a scissor-stair assembly or other enclosure as one exit stairway. For Group R-2, however, the exception permits them to be counted separately when all of these conditions are met: the stair enclosure walls are at least 2-hour fire-resistance-rated and constructed of masonry or masonry equivalent in accordance with department rules; the exit doors are at least 15 feet apart; and the construction separating the two stairs is also at least 2-hour fire-resistance-rated masonry or masonry equivalent.

Your stated 15-foot door separation meets the minimum spacing condition exactly.

### Permitext answer

No. On the stated quantities, the work does not qualify for the less-than-50-percent/less-than-500-square-foot exception. Although 4,000 square feet is 40% of a represented 10,000-square-foot roof, the exception is conjunctive: the area must be both less than 50% of roof area and less than 500 square feet. At 4,000 square feet, it fails the independent 500-square-foot limit. An election to use the 1968 Building Code does not avoid this result: AC 28-101.4.3 expressly directs roof-covering recovery or replacement to BC 1511.1 through 1511.7 and requires compliance with BC 1504.9 unless that two-part exception applies. BC 1511.1 also requires the materials and application methods to comply with Chapter 15.

Accordingly, unless a separate BC 1504.9 exception applies, the recovered/replaced roof covering must meet the reflectance criteria applicable to its slope: for slope no greater than 2:12, either initial solar reflectance 0.70 with thermal emittance 0.75, or SRI 82; for steeper roofs, either initial solar reflectance 0.25 with thermal emittance 0.75, or SRI 39.

Still verify the actual total roof area and actual recovered/replaced area; that the scope is in fact roof recovery or replacement under these provisions; roof slope; the proposed covering/material assembly and its test documentation; and whether any of BC 1504.9's other stated exceptions applies (such as qualifying green-roof, ballasted, equipment-covered, specified material, or other listed conditions). Applicable filing, approval, and inspection requirements also remain unestablished by the supplied passages. The 1968 Code itself was not supplied or searched, but the current enacted AC provision supplied here expressly preserves the cited current roof-work requirements for an optional-1968-Code project.

**What the selected evidence establishes**

- 1968-Code option retains current roof-work provisions: For work involving recovering or replacing an existing roof covering, AC 28-101.4.3 directs the work to BC 1511.1 through 1511.7 and separately requires compliance with BC 1504.9 except under its stated area exception. BC 1511.1 requires materials and methods of application for that work to comply with Chapter 15.
- Area exception requires both thresholds: BC 1511.1 provides that BC 1504.9 does not apply only if the recovery/replacement area is less than 50 percent of the roof area and less than 500 square feet. With the represented areas, 4,000 square feet is below 50 percent of 10,000 square feet but is not below 500 square feet, so the stated work fails the exception.
- Reflectance standards and separate exceptions: BC 1504.9 sets distinct reflectance/emittance or SRI alternatives by roof slope and contains additional enumerated exceptions. Whether one of those exceptions applies depends on the actual roof slope, covering, assembly, and roof conditions.

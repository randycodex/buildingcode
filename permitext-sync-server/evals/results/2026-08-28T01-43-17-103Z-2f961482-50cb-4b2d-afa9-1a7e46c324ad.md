### Permitext answer

Not under Exception 2 merely by adding a draft curtain. Exception 2 is a separate allowance for an exit access stair/ramp serving only between the first and second stories above grade. It requires a building equipped throughout with an automatic sprinkler system complying with BC 903.3.1.1, the minimum number of approved independent exits required by BC 1006 (and never fewer than two means of egress), no opening of those interconnected stories to other stories, and remote location of the unenclosed stair/ramp under BC 1007. A draft curtain is not one of Exception 2’s stated conditions.

The draft-curtain path is Exception 4, not Exception 2. On the supplied text, the open stair may be allowed under Exception 4 only if the building is equipped throughout with a BC 903.3.1.1/NFPA 13 sprinkler system; the vertical-opening area is no more than twice the stair’s horizontal projected area; and the opening has both a draft curtain and closely spaced sprinklers in accordance with NFPA 13 as modified by Appendix Q. If the occupancy is other than Group B or M, the opening also may not connect more than four stories.

Accordingly, installation of the draft curtain alone does not establish permission for the open stair shown. The stated unknowns prevent determining whether either Exception 2 or Exception 4 is satisfied. This also assumes the stair is an exit access stairway and that the occupancy falls within BC 1019.3’s stated scope (other than Groups H, I-2, and I-3).

**What the selected evidence establishes**

- Default enclosure rule: For occupancies other than Groups H, I-2, and I-3, exit access stairways and ramps must be enclosed in a shaft enclosure constructed under Section 713, unless an applicable exception is satisfied.
- Exception 2 does not rest on a draft curtain: Exception 2 permits an unenclosed exit access stairway or ramp only between the first and second stories above grade, in a building equipped throughout with an automatic sprinkler system complying with Section 903.3.1.1, where both stories have the required approved independent exits under Section 1006 and at least two means of egress, the interconnected stories are not open to other stories, and the unenclosed stairway or ramp is remotely located under Section 1007. A draft curtain is not a stated Exception 2 condition.
- Draft-curtain allowance is Exception 4: Exception 4 allows an exit access stairway or ramp in a building equipped throughout with an automatic sprinkler system complying with Section 903.3.1.1 when the vertical-opening area does not exceed twice the horizontal projected area of the stairway or ramp and the opening is protected by both a draft curtain and closely spaced sprinklers in accordance with NFPA 13 as modified by Appendix Q. In occupancies other than Groups B and M, the opening cannot connect more than four stories.
- Required sprinkler-system basis: Where BC 903.3.1.1 is the applicable throughout-sprinkler condition, sprinklers are to be installed throughout in accordance with NFPA 13, subject to the stated code exceptions.

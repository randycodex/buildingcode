### Permitext answer

Yes. For the B, F, S, and qualifying multipurpose-room calculations, apply the appropriate Table 403.1 ratio separately to each occupancy and fixture type, retain the fractional results, sum the fractions across the multiple occupancies, then round the combined total up to the next whole fixture. Ordinarily, divide total occupant load equally between the sexes before applying the ratios; a different distribution is permitted only with approved statistical data.

Yes, conditionally. If the multipurpose room is used for assembly, has an occupant load of fewer than 75 persons, and is accessory to another occupancy, BC 303.1.3 classifies it as Group B or as part of that other occupancy, while permitting its plumbing-fixture count to be calculated under the Assembly requirements. Its resulting Assembly-based fractional fixture requirement can therefore be included in the multiple-occupancy sum before final rounding.

The separate PC 403.1 language supplied for under-75 assembly space concerns building or nonaccessory tenant space classified under a different Building Code provision; it does not supply the basis for this accessory-room conclusion.

The supplied evidence does not establish the actual classifications, occupant loads, applicable Assembly Table 403.1 category and ratios, or whether any existing/shared facilities may be credited. Those facts are needed for a final fixture count, not for the rounding sequence or the conditional Assembly-fixture option.

### Permitext answer

No. The former storage use does not automatically revive merely because it was lawful before July 1, 2008. The existing-building rule allows a lawful use to be continued, subject to retroactive requirements; it does not grant a right to restore a use after a later office change.

A post-July 1, 2008 change of use or occupancy must comply with the code except as otherwise provided by the referenced transition provisions, and a noncompliant change is prohibited and a violation. More specifically, after a change has occurred, re-establishing a prior use that would not be lawful in a new building of the same construction class is prohibited unless and until all applicable code provisions and other applicable laws and rules for that restored use have been met.

The owner must first establish the last-issued certificate of occupancy and the approved documents for the office change; the actual former and proposed occupancy-group and zoning-use-group classifications; whether the storage use is consistent with the last-issued certificate; and whether the proposed storage use would be lawful in a new building of the same construction class. If the storage change is inconsistent with the last-issued certificate—as may be the case if it authorizes office use—a new or amended certificate of occupancy must be issued before the change. If alteration work changes an occupancy group or zoning use group, the space cannot be occupied or used until the commissioner issues a certificate of occupancy after the permitted work is substantially completed in accordance with the approved construction documents and applicable law.

The supplied evidence does not establish the actual certificate terms, classifications, construction class, substantive requirements for the proposed storage use, alteration scope, or applicability of all referenced transition provisions. The stated pre-2008 lawful-storage history is treated here as the owner's representation; records substantiating that representation remain needed for professional reliance.

**What the selected evidence establishes**

If the café is beyond the building line, it must comply not only with Section 3111, but also with the Zoning Resolution, the applicable requirements of the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection and Department of Transportation commissioners, and Chapter 32 projection limitations. The proposed furniture or equipment cannot obstruct the building exit, cellar access hatch, or areaway. A platform may cover a cellar entrance or areaway only if the commissioner permits it upon special application and it is easily removable, prominently designated, designed in accordance with Section 3111.5, and covers an entrance or areaway not used as a required means of egress. The stated proximity to the exit and hatch does not itself establish an obstruction; the layout, clearances, and hatch use are not supplied.

Separate exterior seating is not automatically allowed for the place-of-assembly certificate-of-operation determination. If the sidewalk café is enclosed, its seating must be added to interior seating unless it is separated from the interior seating by fire partitions complying with Section 713. The selected provision does not state the seating-count treatment for a café that is not enclosed.

No. The supplied NYC Building Code section does not prove that HCR requires a bathroom vanity. It applies only where the water closet has a forward-only approach. In that situation, it sets water-closet clearance dimensions and says that a complying lavatory is permitted on the rear wall. The section it cross-references requires an accessible lavatory to comply with ICC A117.1 and specifically permits removable cabinetry below the lavatory if stated conditions are met. That is permission for cabinetry—not a mandate for a vanity.

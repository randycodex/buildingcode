For alterations or additions to an existing system, the new or modified work must meet new-system requirements, but the existing system need not be brought into full compliance with the 2022 Code unless one of BC 901.9.2 through 901.9.6 requires it. The work also cannot leave the installation unsafe, hazardous, or overloaded.

The enlargement itself requires fire-protection systems in the enlarged portion and wherever Chapter 9 would require them in new construction for the space or building. If the resulting Group B occupancy exceeds 100,000 gross square feet and its highest occupied floor is 75 feet or less above the lowest Fire Department vehicle-access level, BC 907.2.2.2 requires automatic smoke detection connected to an automatic fire-alarm system and an emergency voice/alarm communication system that initiates a total-evacuation signal. That is a building/occupancy-wide new-construction trigger in the supplied text; the selected evidence does not say its required features may be confined to the enlarged portion.

Outdoor-dining authorization does not, on this evidence, eliminate Building Code accessibility review: sidewalk cafés and access to them must comply with Chapter 11. If the proposed café is beyond the building line, it must also comply with BC 3111, the Zoning Resolution, applicable Department of Consumer and Worker Protection and Department of Transportation commissioner requirements, and Chapter 32 projection limits. The asserted authorization is not evidence here that those separate requirements have been met.

Café awnings, enclosures, fixtures, equipment, and removable platforms may not obstruct a building exit, cellar access hatch, or areaway. Proximity to an exit or hatch does not alone prove an obstruction, so the proposed layout cannot be found compliant or noncompliant without the relevant geometry. A platform may cover a cellar entrance or areaway only if, upon special application, the commissioner permits an easily removable, prominently designated platform designed under Section 3111.5, and the entrance or areaway is not used as a required means of egress.

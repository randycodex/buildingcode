Carbon monoxide detectors alone do not satisfy that requirement, and the specified carbon-monoxide start point of 35 ppm is higher than the stated required trigger of 25 ppm. The provision also specifies automatic operation at either 25 ppm carbon monoxide or 500 ppb nitrogen dioxide.

The provision separately requires the detectors to be installed in accordance with the manufacturers' instructions. The supplied facts do not establish whether the proposed detector locations, coverage, or installation comply with those instructions. Based only on the selected provision, the conclusion is limited to the described control sequence; it does not establish compliance of the full mechanical-system design or resolve any additional control sequence that may also be proposed.

### Permitext answer

**Conclusion:** Not necessarily. A qualifying accessory assembly multipurpose room with an occupant load of fewer than 75 persons is classified as Group B (or as part of its accessory occupancy), but the selected Building Code provision expressly permits its plumbing-fixture count to be calculated under the Assembly-occupancy requirements instead. The selected Plumbing Code language likewise permits Assembly fixture calculations for a qualifying under-75-person assembly space classified as Group B. Thus, normal Group B fixture requirements may be the applicable baseline based on the Group B classification, but the selected provisions provide a specific permission to use Assembly requirements; they do not establish that Group B fixture ratios must be used. [BC §303.1.3, Passage e4eb837e-309d-475d-8b7a-92ddd6243f0b; PC §403.1, Passage 0031d9db-393b-47a7-b0b3-f4a56351480f]

PC §403.1 requires fixtures by occupancy type and Table 403.1, with occupant load and occupancy classification determined under the NYC Building Code. For the room described, the relevant project-specific classification issue is whether it is an assembly-purpose room, accessory to another occupancy, with an occupant load below 75. If so, BC §303.1.3 classifies it as Group B or part of the accessory occupancy, while expressly allowing its fixture number to be calculated using Assembly requirements. The parallel PC provision also states that an under-75-person assembly building or nonaccessory tenant space classified as Group B under the cited Building Code exception is permitted to use Assembly requirements. [PC §403.1, Passage 23983c25-01eb-4d43-a7b4-f6b2103335e8; BC §303.1.3, Passage e4eb837e-309d-475d-8b7a-92ddd6243f0b; PC §403.1, Passage 0031d9db-393b-47a7-b0b3-f4a56351480f]

If a fixture calculation is performed, the selected procedure requires: (1) determine the total occupant load under the NYC Building Code; (2) ordinarily divide that load equally between sexes; (3) apply the applicable Table 403.1 fixture ratio(s) to each sex; and (4) round each resulting fractional fixture count up. For multiple occupancies, sum the fractional results for each occupancy before rounding up. The 50/50 sex split is not required where approved statistical data supports another distribution. A special floor-total rule applies to Group B office occupancies. [PC §403.1.1, Passages 85aca34d-08ef-412f-8701-32128b9408f2 and 059c4c69-d6a5-41de-976a-b7923f15eb64]

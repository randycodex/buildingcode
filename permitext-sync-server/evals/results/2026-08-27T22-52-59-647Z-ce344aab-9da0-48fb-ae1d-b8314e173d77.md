### Permitext answer

No—not on the stated basis. Because the seats are movable rather than fixed, the fixed-seat method does not apply. Each area without fixed seating must have an occupant load no lower than its floor area divided by the Table 1004.1.3 factor for that area’s actual function. Counting only selected movable seats therefore does not establish the required design occupant load.

The conference, soft-seating, and coffee-break/lounge functions cannot simply be omitted as nonsimultaneous. Where an area contains functions with different occupant-load factors, each function’s floor area must be calculated independently. The supplied provisions contain no nonsimultaneous-use reduction. A lower basis is available only if the commissioner establishes one where actual occupancy will be significantly below the table result. Since no such commissioner-established basis is identified, the existing 1:100 documentation cannot be retained merely by proposal or by seat counting.

A final revised load cannot be calculated from the supplied evidence because the applicable Table 1004.1.3 factors are not included, and the room-by-room areas, actual functions, layouts, and simultaneous-use conditions remain unresolved. The supplied evidence also does not establish whether the existing 1:100 basis matches the factor for every proposed function or whether the resulting load is supportable by the available egress capacity.

**What the selected evidence establishes**

- Multiple functions require separate area calculations: When an area under consideration contains multiple functions with different occupant-load factors, the design occupant load must be based on the floor area of each function calculated independently. Thus, the conference, soft-seating, and coffee-break/lounge areas must be evaluated by their actual functions when their applicable factors differ; the provision does not supply a nonsimultaneous-use exclusion.
- Movable-seat areas use the applicable floor-area factor: For an area without fixed seating, occupant load must be not less than the result of dividing the floor area under consideration by the Table 1004.1.3 factor assigned to the function of that space. The stated movable seating does not meet the supplied fixed-seating rule, so counting selected movable seats is not a substitute for this minimum calculation.
- A lower basis requires commissioner action: Where the actual number of occupants will be significantly lower than the Table 1004.1.3 result, the commissioner may establish a lower basis. The stated scenario identifies no commissioner-established lower basis, so this provision does not support retaining a lower load by applicant documentation alone.
- The seat-count method is confined to fixed seats: For areas having fixed seats and aisles, occupant load is determined by the number of fixed seats installed. Areas without fixed seating, including waiting spaces, are instead determined under Section 1004.1.3 and added to the fixed-seat count. Because the stated seats are movable, this fixed-seat method does not authorize the proposed selected-seat count.

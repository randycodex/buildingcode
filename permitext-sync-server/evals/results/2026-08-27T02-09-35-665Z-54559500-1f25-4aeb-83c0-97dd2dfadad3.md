### Permitext answer

The owner’s claim is not supported by these passages. If the proposed café is beyond the building line, it must comply not only with Building Code Section 3111 but also with the Zoning Resolution, the applicable Department of Consumer and Worker Protection and Department of Transportation commissioner requirements, and Chapter 32 projection limits. Thus, an outdoor-dining authorization does not, on this evidence, displace those separate requirements.

For a sidewalk café, furniture, equipment, enclosures, removable platforms, and similar items may not obstruct a building exit or a cellar access hatch/areaway. The only supplied exception for covering a cellar entrance or areaway is conditional: upon special application, the commissioner may permit an easily removable, prominently designated platform designed under Section 3111.5, and only where that entrance or areaway is not used as a required means of egress. The submitted geometry is therefore insufficient to determine compliance at the exit and hatch.

Accessibility review is not eliminated: sidewalk cafés and their access must comply with Chapter 11. Where dining surfaces are provided, at least 10 percent of the total seating and standing spaces, with at least one of each type of dining surface, must be accessible, distributed throughout the facility, and on a level reached by an accessible route. The supplied evidence does not establish the total or types of dining surfaces, their accessible locations, or the access route, so a compliant accessible count and distribution cannot be determined.

Exterior seats are not automatically counted separately for the stated assembly-seating issue. If the café is enclosed, its seating must be added to the indoor seating when determining whether a place-of-assembly certificate of operation is required, unless the two are separated by fire partitions complying with Section 713. Because enclosure status and the claimed fire-partition separation are unknown, the applicable counting result remains unresolved. The supplied passage does not state a corresponding aggregation rule for an unenclosed café.
